# Rule sources

What each rule family rests on, from the sources themselves and the research behind
[`detection-catalogue.md`](detection-catalogue.md). **A rule without a source does not load**,
so this page is where a rule starts; a course lab manual is a good source.

"Observable" is how well the rule can be checked from a robot script and the
names of its liquids alone.

## Where the sources disagree

| question | one reading | the other | what we do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reusing a tip down a serial dilution | Integra: fine for aqueous liquids if you blow out after each mix | Svec et al. used a fresh tip at every step | A warning that quotes both |
| Ethanol for bead washes | Beckman: 70% | Illumina: 80%, with about 2 min air-dry | Check that there are two washes in fresh ethanol, not the percentage |
| Ice or a cold block | WHO accepts either | UK SMI prefers a cold block, because ice can contaminate | Not a rule |
| Bead dry time | Illumina's 2011 TruSeq guide: at least 15 minutes | NEB and Illumina's current pages: up to 5 minutes, about 2 typical, do not over-dry | Check that there is a dry step, not its length |
| Undiluted cDNA in a qPCR | Qiagen: no more than 10% of the reaction | Thermo: up to 20% | Warn above 20%; note between |
| Setting up PCR | Non-hot-start: on ice, polymerase last, straight to a preheated block (NEB, Thermo) | Hot-start: "completely inactive at room temperature", set up at the bench (Thermo-Start) | Time-at-ambient rules exempt a mix whose name says hot-start |
| Returning the disposal volume to the stock | Opentrons offers `blowout_location="source well"` and 48 library protocols blow out into a stock after pre-mixing it | A clinical lab traced NTC contamination to a hard-coded blow-out (labautomation.io 7281) | A note when the tip is clean; the contamination rules when it is not |

## What we looked for and did not find

Each of these needs a source before it can be a rule. Our lab manuals
are the likely place. Found since the first read: how many minutes beads take to settle
(Opentrons: 5 and 7), a plain sentence to change tips between samples (Promega,
Illumina), a room-temperature limit for restriction enzymes (a week, Clark 2000).

* "Never add an enzyme to unbuffered water."

* Evaporation from an open plate on a robot deck, per hour, at room temperature.

* How many minutes cells or beads take to settle in a tube.

* A rate of wrong-well placement during manual setup in a research lab.
* An omission rate for NTC or no-RT controls in published qPCR.
* An ELISA "do not let the plate dry" threshold in minutes.

## Carry-over: what may be carried into what

The contamination rules know that a used tip carried *something* into a well. What it
was decides how much it matters, and this table decides that (D-20). The roles come
from the name the protocol gives in `define_liquid`, matched against the word lists in
`provette/rules/roles.yaml`; **we own those lists** and extend them from
the names we meet in real protocols, one word per line. A name the lists cannot place
changes nothing: the finding keeps the severity the structure alone gave it, and the
report names the liquid as unplaced so it can be taught.

| carried | into | severity | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| a carrier (water, PBS, buffer, medium, ethanol, DMSO) | anything | note | inert; a trace of diluent in a stock about to be diluted with it changes nothing measurable |
| a sample (DNA, cDNA, colony, library, serum, a standard, a drug, a plasmid) | anything | what the structure says: error if the stock is drawn from again and the trace spreads, warning otherwise | one sample in another, or in the shared master mix, is the failure this project exists for |
| a reagent (master mix, enzyme, primer, dye, beads, antibody) | a sample or another reagent | at most a warning | master mix in a template tube spoils that tube for later, but does not spread through a plate the way a sample in the mix does |
| a reagent | a carrier | at most a warning | same |
| anything undeclared or unplaced | anything | unchanged | the rule cannot know, and says so (`roles_basis: none`) |

The case the table exists for is a diluent tip entering the stock it is about to dilute: a
note. Every sample-into-stock finding keeps the structural severity.
